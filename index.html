These are the ground rules and expectations for the QAI × Debrah Kobby FX mentorship. They exist to keep sessions productive, focused, and valuable for everyone involved. Please read through before your first session.
Attendance & Punctuality
01
Be on time
Join the Zoom call at least 2 minutes before the scheduled start. Sessions begin promptly and will not be held up for late joiners.
02
Attend all 12 sessions
Each session builds on the last. If you must miss one, notify the mentor at least 24 hours in advance. Missing more than 2 sessions without notice may result in removal from the program.
03
Camera on
Cameras are encouraged during sessions. It keeps engagement high and helps mentors gauge understanding in real time.
Conduct & Respect
04
No talking over the mentor
Mute yourself when not speaking. Use the raise-hand feature or drop questions in chat — they will be addressed during Q&A segments.
05
Respect all participants
No judgment, no mockery. Everyone is at a different level. Questions are encouraged — there are no stupid ones in this program.
06
No sharing of session content
All materials, recordings, and strategies shared in this program are private. Do not redistribute PDFs, recordings, or notes outside the group.
Preparation & Engagement
07
Come prepared
Review notes from the previous session before each new one. If homework or chart analysis was assigned, have it ready to discuss.
08
Have your trading platform open
TradingView or your broker platform must be open during sessions so you can follow along with live chart analysis in real time.
09
Practice between sessions
Use a demo account every day. Consistency outside sessions matters more than anything that happens inside them.
Expectations
10
No live trading pressure
You are not expected to trade live during this program. Focus on learning and demo trading. Risk real capital only when you are consistently profitable on demo.
11
Ask questions actively
This program only works if you engage. If something is unclear, ask immediately. Do not let confusion pile up from session to session.
12
Honest feedback goes both ways
Mentors will give honest assessments of your progress. You are also encouraged to give feedback on sessions so the program can keep improving.
✏   These rules will be updated by the mentors as the program develops. Check back before each week begins.
